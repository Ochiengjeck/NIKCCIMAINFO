Below is a **complete structural breakdown of the Public-Facing Website** for
the **Nigeria-Kenya Chamber of Commerce, Industry, Mines, and Agriculture (NiKCCIMA)**.

This is not a brochure site.
It must function as a **bilateral trade corridor interface** and institutional credibility engine.



# 1. STRATEGIC POSITIONING OF THE PUBLIC WEBSITE

The public platform must:

1. Establish institutional legitimacy
2. Attract investors & SMEs
3. Convert visitors into paying members
4. Demonstrate AfCFTA corridor execution
5. Serve as a structured trade gateway between Nigeria and Kenya

It should communicate clearly:

* This is a governed institution
* It is operational, not ceremonial
* It produces measurable trade outcomes



# 2. DESIGN FOUNDATION

### Visual Identity

* Primary colors: **Green and Red**
* Accent: Neutral white/black typography
* Institutional tone (not flashy, not startup-ish)
* Diplomatic, structured layout

### Design Characteristics

* Wide hero sections
* Structured grids
* KPI highlight cards
* Subtle motion (not excessive animation)
* Clear call-to-action buttons
